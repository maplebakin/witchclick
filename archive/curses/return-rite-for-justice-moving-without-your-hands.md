## Opening Reflection

There is a quiet, almost startling moment when you realize justice no longer needs your effort. The story that once demanded your vigilance begins to unfold on its own, shaped by the weight of their choices rather than your endurance. This rite is not about revenge or poetic symmetry. It is about stepping out of the way so truth can travel on its natural path, unassisted by your pain or your hope. You release the role you were never meant to play, and allow consequence, clarity, and accountability to rise without your involvement.

## Invocation

Let justice rise without calling on my energy.

## Method

Place a candle before you and leave it unlit for now. Sit with your palms open on your knees, letting the stillness gather. Imagine the threads of the situation—every action, every dismissal, every lie or distortion—stretching outward from the person who wove them. With each slow breath, feel those threads loosening from your shoulders. Whisper softly that you no longer carry the responsibility of correction or exposure. Picture the candle as a small, neutral beacon, not of judgment, but of unfolding truth. Now imagine the threads drifting toward it, drawn not to you but to their natural point of resolution. Light the candle with a single calm breath. As the flame steadies, visualize justice moving independently of your will, guided by the integrity of the world rather than your exhaustion. When your breath feels lighter, extinguish the flame gently, signaling your release from the story’s momentum.

## Closure & Aftercare

Rest your hands over your heart for a few breaths. You have stepped out of a role that drained you, allowing the situation to resolve without your intervention. Justice does not require your watchfulness or your sacrifice. Let your body soften into this truth and return to the rhythm of your own life. You are no longer entangled in the outcome. You are free to rest.

## Safety Notes

Avoid imagining specific punishment. Keep breath steady. Stop if emotions escalate.
