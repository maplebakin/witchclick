## Opening Reflection

There are spells that form without candles or incantations—subtle narratives, soft manipulations, quiet assurances that only work as long as you believe them. This reflection honors the moment you feel that belief slip, like a thread finally relaxing after being held too tight. When the spell breaks, the air shifts. Smoke rises through what once held you, revealing how little power it carried on its own. In this rite, you do not seek revenge; you simply let the structure collapse under its own emptiness. What returns is your clarity, unbound and whole.

## Invocation

Let the broken spell dissolve and return its remnants to their origin.

## Method

Sit in a dim space where the light feels soft and unintrusive. Place a small bowl or cup in front of you—something simple, steady, and cool beneath your fingertips. Imagine a quiet plume of smoke rising from its center, carrying the remnants of the spell that once held you only because you believed in it. Notice how the smoke curls without force, how easily it lifts once your conviction withdraws. Inhale slowly, reclaiming the part of yourself that had been entwined in that old narrative. Exhale with purpose, guiding the smoke away from your body and back toward the source of its creation. Speak softly about the feeling of the spell breaking—the moment you realized its structure thinned and its grip loosened. You do not need to speak names or events. Focus on sensation: the lightness, the shift, the clarity. Touch the bowl’s rim to anchor your breath. Let the smoke gather height, then drift back across the invisible tether to the one who cast it, returning only what belongs to them. When the final wisp fades in your mind, release one long exhale to seal the unraveling.

## Closure & Aftercare

Stay seated for a moment as the imagined smoke clears. Feel the quiet of a spell that no longer holds shape around you. The break was not destruction—only truth stepping forward. What returns to you now is spaciousness, a steadier breath, the certainty that your belief is yours to give or withdraw. When you rise, carry the knowledge that nothing can bind you once your conviction steps away.

## Safety Notes

If the memory feels sharp, pause and ground through slow exhales.
