## Opening Reflection

There is a quiet inevitability when a truth someone buried begins to surface. It does not claw its way upward or arrive with spectacle. It simply rises, shaped by the weight of what was denied. This rite is not for coaxing confession or forcing revelation. It is for stepping out of the path of what is unfolding, letting the truth find its own momentum without carrying the emotional cost they left behind. You acknowledge the sting of what they hid, but you release the burden of holding their silence or shielding them from their own choices.

## Invocation

Let their buried truth return without touching my field.

## Method

Place a small bowl of water before you, still and clear. Sit with your breath steady and let the room settle around you. Imagine the truth they buried sinking far beneath the surface of this water—heavy, dense, waiting. With each slow inhale, feel the distance between you and that weight growing. Whisper that what belongs to them will rise on its own, without your effort or involvement. Dip your fingertips lightly into the bowl and stir once, counterclockwise, envisioning the buried truth loosening from where they pressed it down. Picture it drifting upward like a faint shimmer, not toward you but toward the surface of the water, where it becomes visible by its own force. Lift your fingers and let the droplets fall back into the bowl naturally. Cover the bowl with a clean cloth, acknowledging that the resurfacing is not yours to witness or interpret. You are simply returning the truth to its rightful path.

## Closure & Aftercare

Rest your palms over your heart and let your shoulders soften. You are free from carrying the silence they shaped. Their truth will move as it needs to, without your guidance or grief. Sit in the lightness that follows release, trusting that clarity always finds its way to the surface. You return to your own life, unburdened.

## Safety Notes

Avoid visualizing outcomes. Breathe slowly. Stop the rite if emotions become sharp or overwhelming.
