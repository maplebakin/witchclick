## Opening Reflection

There is a heavy pause when someone begins feeling the weight of their own patterns. You can sense the shift before they do, a slow recognition forming where denial once lived. This rite is not about condemning them or celebrating their struggle. It is simply the moment you stop carrying what they created and allow the gravity of their choices to return to its rightful source. By witnessing without absorbing, you reclaim your clarity and release the burden of trying to fix what was never yours.

## Invocation

Let their patterns settle back into their origin.

## Method

Take two small objects or scraps of paper. Let one stand for a responsibility that belongs to you, and the other for a consequence of someone else's choices. Name each out loud or in writing. Hold both for a moment and notice that carrying them together does not make them equally yours. Put your own object somewhere you can reach it. Place the other on a separate surface, or write 'not mine to manage' beneath it. Identify one concrete act you can stop doing on their behalf, if it is safe to do so. You do not need to predict whether they will change, suffer, apologize, or even notice. This return is the decision to stop doing the carrying; it does not control what happens to anyone else.

## Closure & Aftercare

Cover the stone with a cloth and set it aside. Allow your breath to lengthen and your shoulders to soften. You are not responsible for how they respond to their own accumulated weight. Your work ends with release, not prediction. Let the space around you feel lighter as you return to your own rhythm. Rest in the quiet clarity that comes from stepping out of their pattern’s gravity.

## Safety Notes

Stay grounded. Do not imagine outcomes. Pause if you feel emotionally overwhelmed.
