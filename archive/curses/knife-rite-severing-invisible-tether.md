## Opening Reflection

Realizing there has always been an invisible tether between you and another person can feel both shocking and strangely familiar. You notice how your choices seem to lean in their direction, even when they are not in the room. This rite is for the moment you finally see the line that has been tugging at you all along. You are not here to destroy connection; you are here to cut what was claimed without your consent, and return that pull to its rightful owner.

## Invocation

Let the unseen tether surface, sever, and return its claim.

## Method

Choose a quiet space where you will not be interrupted. Sit with your spine supported and place a small object before you, something that feels neutral and steady. Close your eyes and sense for the invisible tether between you and this person. Notice where it seems to hook into your body: throat, chest, belly, back. As you inhale, gather your awareness around that point. As you exhale, let a ceremonial knife appear in your inner hand, forged from clarity and refusal. Hold the imagined blade to the tether and speak aloud how this unseen pull has shaped your choices. Do not name them; describe only the pattern. When your breath feels strong and present, cut through the tether in one clean motion. Watch your end dissolve into light, while their end coils back toward them, carrying its own weight home. Finish with three slow breaths, sealing your field.

## Closure & Aftercare

Stay seated as your nervous system resets to the absence of that hidden pull. Notice any warmth, tingling, or lightness where the tether once sat. You have not sworn off connection; you have ended an arrangement that depended on you staying unaware. When you stand, imagine your edges smoothing but not hardening, available for mutual, chosen bonds instead of unseen claim. For the next day or two, move gently and drink water often, letting your body learn how freedom feels.

## Safety Notes

If you feel unsteady afterward, ground with breath or water before big decisions.
