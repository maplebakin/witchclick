## Opening Reflection

Truth has a way of rising even when everyone pretends the air is still. It gathers in the quiet corners first, building a pressure that invites the unspoken to take form. In this reflection, you honor the moment just before revelation, when silence begins to betray what it once protected. You are not forcing confession; you are allowing honesty to find its own path upward. This practice steadies your breath while letting hidden motives lift into the light that has been waiting for them.

## Invocation

May truth rise cleanly from silence and reflect their intent without distortion.

## Method

Set a small mirror before you, choosing one that feels unburdened by old stories. Light a single candle if it helps anchor your attention, though the flame is optional. Sit where your back feels supported and allow your breath to settle into an easy rhythm. Bring the person to mind whose silence has obscured the truth you needed. You are not calling them in; you are acknowledging the shape their quiet left around you.

With each inhale, imagine the mirror gathering the pressure of what went unsaid. With each exhale, picture that pressure rising, like steam lifting from a cooling cup. Speak softly to the mirror, naming the moments their silence shifted the air, the choices that created confusion, and the weight you carried because of what was withheld. Let your voice be steady, not sharp.

Then gently turn the mirror outward. Envision truth lifting from silence and becoming visible to those who must understand it. This is not exposure for punishment; it is clarity returning to its rightful place. When you feel the work complete, extinguish the candle or rest your hands on the mirror’s back so the energy can settle.

## Closure & Aftercare

Let the mirror rest face-down and breathe into the quiet that follows. You have not sought harm, only the unveiling of what silence once concealed. As truth rises, allow yourself to release the tension you held on its behalf. Touch something grounding—a stone, a cup, your own pulse—to remind your body that clarity is safe. When you are ready, close the ritual by affirming that whatever returns will find only its rightful origin.

## Safety Notes

Perform this ritual only when calm enough to stay grounded and focused.
