## Opening Reflection

Some past actions do not stay buried, no matter how carefully someone tried to hide their shape. They press forward through the present, surfacing in gestures, glances, and the way a room shifts when their name is spoken. This reflection honors the moment when consequences finally meet their originator, not as punishment but as balance reasserting itself. You stand witness without forcing anything, letting the truth of their history rise into view. Here, silence becomes porous, and the echoes they created return in unmistakable form.

## Invocation

Let their past step forward and meet them without disguise.

## Method

Place a mirror where it can stand upright, steady and unembellished. Sit before it and breathe until your shoulders loosen. Think of the person whose past has begun to seep into the present, not to summon them, but to acknowledge the consequences that have followed them here. Let the mirror warm under your gaze. With each inhale, imagine it gathering faint impressions of the choices they once made. With each exhale, picture those choices standing a little nearer to the surface.

Speak softly to the mirror, naming the patterns you witnessed without exaggeration. Describe the ways their past shaped your interactions, the strain it placed on your trust, and the signals you ignored out of hope or courtesy. As your voice steadies, imagine the mirror clarifying like a pond losing its murk. The purpose is recognition, not vengeance.

When the moment feels full, turn the mirror outward. Envision their past revealing itself in present time—visible to those who need clarity, impossible for them to sidestep. Let this unfold only to the degree that truth requires. When finished, place the mirror face-down so the energy can rest.

## Closure & Aftercare

Let the mirror lie still and breathe into the space that opens around you. What has risen is not yours to carry any longer; it simply returns to its rightful path. You have named the truth without twisting it, allowing their past to stand where it belongs. Ground yourself by touching something cool or steady, reminding your body that the work is complete. When you are ready, close your eyes briefly and release any residue that is not yours.

## Safety Notes

Use this ritual only when calm and grounded enough to stay emotionally steady.
