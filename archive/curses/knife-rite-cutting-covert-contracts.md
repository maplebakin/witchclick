## Opening Reflection

There is a cold weight in realizing someone has been keeping score in shadows—quiet expectations, unspoken debts, the kind of covert contract that whispers ‘you owe me’ even when you never agreed. This reflection honors the bitterness of discovering those terms after the fact. You are not here to retaliate; you are here to cut cleanly through obligations that were never yours to carry. In this rite, the knife represents truth sharpened, severing the hidden agreements woven into your connection and returning their weight to the hands that shaped them.

## Invocation

Let the unseen contracts be cut and returned to their maker.

## Method

Sit in a quiet space with dim, steady light. Place a small object before you—something firm, cool, and honest in its weight. Close your eyes and imagine the covert contracts laid across your chest like brittle parchment, each inscribed with expectations you never consented to. Inhale deeply, gathering your strength into the center of your body. As you exhale, picture a slender luminous knife forming in your grasp—an edge of clarity, not harm. Lift this imagined blade and slice through one contract at a time. Hear the faint crack as each hidden obligation breaks. Speak softly about how each expectation felt in your body: the guilt, the tension, the pressure to comply. Do not name the person; speak only of the feeling. As each cut is made, watch the fragmented edges lift into smoke and drift back toward their rightful origin. What was never yours dissolves. What remains is your autonomy. When the final contract curls into ashlight, send one long exhale to seal the severing.

## Closure & Aftercare

Sit with the quiet expansion in your chest. Feel how different the air tastes when no secret debts cling to your breath. You have not cut connection—you have cut coercion. Your yes and no belong only to you now. Rise gently, knowing that nothing unspoken binds you anymore, and the weight of those covert expectations has returned to the one who crafted them.

## Safety Notes

If guilt rises, pause and breathe slowly until the body softens.
