## Opening Reflection

Some truths rise like steam from a pot left unwatched, revealing themselves when the room grows quiet enough to listen. In this reflection, you let the air thicken with honesty until the sheen of someone’s intentions stands out against the walls. No force, no harm—just the slow illumination that comes when pretense can’t keep its shape. You stand steady in that glow, noticing what was hidden and how the concealment altered your own breath. This practice clears the haze without demanding apologies, allowing clarity to settle into its rightful place.

## Invocation

Let their concealed intent gleam openly, returning truth to its unguarded surface.

## Method

Place a simple mirror before you, nothing ornate, just a clear surface that can hold a moment’s honesty. Sit where your breath feels unhurried. Imagine the person whose intentions have muddied your sense of ease. You are not summoning them; you are acknowledging the imprint their behavior left on your field. With each slow inhale, picture the mirror warming, as though it gathers the residue of what was said, implied, or avoided. With each exhale, allow the surface to sharpen.

Now speak quietly—not to them, but to the truth itself. Name the patterns you witnessed, without embellishment. Say how their choices shaped the room, how the tone shifted, and how your body responded. As you speak, invite the mirror to clarify rather than condemn. The goal is revelation, not ruin.

When you feel the moment crest, turn the mirror outward. Imagine their intentions becoming visible to anyone who needs to sense them. Not exposed in humiliation, but rendered unmistakable. Let the clarity travel only as far as justice allows, and no further. When finished, place the mirror face-down so the work can cool.

## Closure & Aftercare

Let the mirror rest, and let your breath soften. You have not cursed a person; you have honored the truth of what their actions set in motion. Clarity is its own consequence, and you have chosen to witness it without drowning in it. As the echoes fade, ground yourself by touching something steady—a stone, a piece of fabric, the floor beneath you. Let the moment close gently, knowing you returned only what was already theirs.

## Safety Notes

Keep focus on your grounding; avoid performing this ritual while highly distressed.
