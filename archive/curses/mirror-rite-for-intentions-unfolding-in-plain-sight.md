## Opening Reflection

There is a strange steadiness that comes when someone's hidden motives begin slipping into view. You feel the subtle shift first, that faint distortion in their words where the truth presses against the surface. When intentions become visible to others, you are no longer the only one sensing the mismatch. This rite is not about exposing them, but about letting clarity settle where confusion once lived. You step back from their projection and let the world see what it will, without you carrying the burden.

## Invocation

Let their intentions reveal themselves without my involvement.

## Method

Sit with a small mirror in a quiet corner where the air feels still. Place it upright before you, angled just enough to catch a soft shape rather than a full reflection. Imagine the person's presence as a faint outline in the glass, blurred at the edges. With each calm breath, picture the space around that outline brightening, not to accuse, but to clarify contrast. Their intentions gather like loose threads, and instead of tangling in you, they drift outward toward the mirror's surface. Lift your hand and trace a slow, deliberate line down the center of the glass, symbolizing your separation from their story. Whisper that their motives belong to them alone and will be seen in their own time. Let the mirror hold what is theirs, while your breath steadies you in your own field. When the air around you feels cleaner, lower your hand and let the reflection fade.

## Closure & Aftercare

Turn the mirror face-down and rest your palms on your thighs. Let your breath deepen until the tension dissolves. You have stepped out of the space where their intentions once pressed against you. You are not responsible for how or when others perceive the truth. Your task is complete as soon as you release the weight of interpreting their behavior. Let your body soften back into itself and trust the clarity that follows.

## Safety Notes

Keep attention on your breath. Avoid imagining specific outcomes. Stop if tension rises beyond comfort.
