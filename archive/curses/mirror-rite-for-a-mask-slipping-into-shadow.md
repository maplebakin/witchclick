## Opening Reflection

There is a quiet ache that rises when someone’s polished image begins to fray. You sense the truth beneath their practiced expressions, the uneven seams where their shadow seeps through the mask they insist on wearing. This reflection is not about exposing them to the world, but about steadying yourself in the presence of contradiction. You do not chase their darkness or excuse it; you simply refuse to carry what is not yours. This rite helps you return their projection and reclaim the edges of your own clarity.

## Invocation

Let the hidden shape quietly return to its source.

## Method

Sit before a small mirror in soft light. Place one hand over your chest and let your breath settle until the room’s hum feels steady. Notice the image reflected: not their face, but the faint impression they leave in you. Imagine the mask they show the world hovering lightly on the glass, thin as fog. With a slow exhale, allow the shadow behind it to reveal its outline—not to judge, but to understand where it ends and where you begin. When you feel the distinction, trace a gentle circle on the mirror’s surface with one fingertip. Whisper that the energy is not yours to hold. Visualize it sliding away like water, returning to the one who generated it. Keep your breath steady. Keep your tone soft. The goal is release, not unraveling. When the mirror feels clearer, close your eyes and let the final residue fall away.

## Closure & Aftercare

When the rite settles, lower your shoulders and place the mirror face-down for a moment. Let your breath lengthen. You have returned what was never meant to sit inside you. There is no need to follow its path or imagine its impact. Your work ends here, in the calm that forms when projection loosens its grip. Rest in the simplicity of that release.

## Safety Notes

Stop if you feel overwhelmed. Keep focus on your breath. Direct no energy toward anyone.
