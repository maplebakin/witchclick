## Opening Reflection

There is a distinct shift when truth begins to rise from a place that has held it quiet for too long. You can feel the pressure change in the air, like a room exhaling after holding itself tight. This ritual is not about exposing anyone or forcing revelation. It is about allowing clarity to surface where distortion once lived, and letting the silence that protected someone else’s narrative dissolve. As truth gathers its own momentum, you step out of the way and let it speak without carrying the cost of its emergence.

## Invocation

Let the truth rise without my silence holding it.

## Method

Sit beside a mirror in dim, steady light. Place the mirror so it reflects only a sliver of your outline, leaving most of its surface open and unclaimed. Close your eyes and sense the weight of the silence you have held—its shape, its temperature, its subtle ache. With a slow breath, imagine that silence thinning, becoming translucent. Visualize truth beneath it, quiet but persistent, rising like a faint glow from deep water. Lift your hand and rest your fingertips lightly on the mirror’s edge; do not trace symbols, only acknowledge the boundary between what is yours and what never was. Whisper that the silence you kept is released, and whatever truth belongs to the situation may rise on its own terms. As you breathe, let the mirror become the place where unspoken things gather their form, entirely outside your responsibility. When the air around you feels clearer, lower your hand and sit for a moment in the absence of weight.

## Closure & Aftercare

Turn the mirror face-down and let your shoulders soften. You are no longer the keeper of someone else's quiet. Truth will move as it needs to, without your holding or shaping. Your part is to step back into your own steadiness, letting the burden of silence fall away. Rest in the space that opens when you stop carrying what was never yours, trusting the clarity that follows.

## Safety Notes

Keep focus on breath. Release specifics. Pause if emotions tighten. Direct no energy toward anyone.
