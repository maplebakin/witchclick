steps:

    duration: 2 minutes
    description: "Fill a glass of water. As you sip, imagine the water clearing static from your throat and chest."
  - title: Sound Cocoon
    duration: 6 minutes
    description: "Put on your headphones and start the rain track. Close your eyes and imagine the sound falling in a circle around you. Inhale for four counts, hold for two, and exhale for six."
  - title: Aura Sweep
    duration: 3 minutes
    description: "With eyes still closed, sweep your hands slowly from crown to feet, about six inches from your body, three times. Picture the rain rinsing any residue that doesn't belong."
  - title: Re-entry
    duration: 1 minute
    description: "Remove headphones, stretch your arms overhead, and name one word for how you want to feel in the next block of time."
