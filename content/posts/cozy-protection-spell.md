# Opening Reflection

Protection doesn’t need thunder and brimstone; it can be a knit blanket, a warm mug, and a clear intention.  
Before we begin, check in with your body: jaw unclenched, shoulders down, breath steady.

---

## Quick Reset (1–2 minutes)

- Inhale for 4, hold 2, exhale for 6 — three rounds.
- Imagine your skin as the boundary of a lantern. You are the warm light *inside*.

---

## Materials (keep it simple)

- A tealight or LED candle  
- A **Clear Quartz** (or any pocket crystal you love)  
- A notebook or **journal** for a one-line intention  
- A cozy drink (optional but delightful)

> Tip: If you don’t have a crystal yet, start with a small **starter crystal set** and choose the smooth stone that feels best in your hand.

---

## Steps

1. **Spark** — Light the candle. Whisper: *“I choose warmth and clear boundaries.”*  
2. **Anchor** — Hold the crystal. Notice its temperature, shape, tiny glints.  
3. **Write** — In your notebook, one sentence: *“Today, my energy is mine.”*  
4. **Trace** — With your fingertip, draw a gentle circle around the candle (no touching the flame).  
5. **Seal** — Close the notebook and place it beneath the candle holder for the session.

Let the candle burn safely (or keep the LED on) for at least 9 minutes.

---

## Safety First

- Keep open flames attended and on a stable, non-flammable surface.  
- If you’re anxious, use an LED candle — intention > aesthetics, always.

---

## Aftercare

- Slip the crystal in your pocket or bag.  
- Re-read your one-line intention later today.  
- If the day was heavy, repeat the **Quick Reset** and step 5 (Seal).

---

## Why Clear Quartz?

Clear Quartz is a friendly “amplifier” and a neutral pairing stone. It helps anchor simple, repeatable rituals without overcomplication.

---

## Keep Going (cozy upgrades)

- A dotted **notebook** makes bite-sized intentions feel tidy.  
- Start a tiny shelf altar: candle, crystal, postcard — nothing fancy needed.
