steps:
  - title: Settle & Breathe
    duration: 2 minutes
    description: "Sit somewhere you can stay undisturbed. Close your eyes, lengthen your spine, and take four slow inhales and exhales, counting to five each way."
  - title: Phrase the Intention
    duration: 2 minutes
    description: "Write one present-tense sentence that captures what you need, like 'I move through this sprint with clear focus.' Remove vowels and duplicate letters to create your sigil skeleton."
  - title: Draw the Sigil
    duration: 4 minutes
    description: "Combine the remaining letters into a single mark. Trust your hand. Let lines overlap and curve until the design feels charged."
  - title: Charge & Close
    duration: 2 minutes
    description: "Place the sigil over your heart or forehead. Inhale deeply and visualize the symbol lighting up. Exhale and imagine it sealing into your body. Fold the paper and keep it near your workspace or tuck it away to release the outcome."
