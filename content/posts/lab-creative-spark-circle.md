steps:
  - title: Warm Welcome
    duration: 5 minutes
    description: "Gather the group in video or in-person. Each person names one tiny creative win and a focus for the next half hour. Pick a playlist that suits the mood—lyrical if you like sing-alongs, instrumental if you need fewer words."
  - title: Momentum Burst
    duration: 20 minutes
    description: "Start the timer. Everyone mutes or quiets themselves but keeps video on if that feels motivating. Encourage gentle micro-movements or stretches to keep energy flowing."
  - title: Reflection Round
    duration: 7 minutes
    description: "When the timer ends, invite each participant to share what shifted. Celebrate partial progress and hidden breakthroughs alike."
  - title: Close & Schedule
    duration: 3 minutes
    description: "Choose the next co-work window while momentum is high. Add it to a shared calendar or thread so the commitment stays alive."
