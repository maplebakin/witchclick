## Calm that honours movement

Calm is not absence—it is capacity. In the Calm hub we build steady rhythms that keep us resourced for the movements we love. When the world feels jagged, these rituals offer a tactile reminder that we can soften without disengaging. You will find grounding scripts that do not bypass grief, playlists that match different nervous-system states, and partner offerings that redistribute care.

## Practices you will meet

- **Community soothe map.** Chart the people, places, and practices that help you downshift when the overwhelm spikes.
- **Repair scripts.** Templates for re-opening a conversation after conflict, complete with body check-ins to keep everyone resourced.
- **Mutual aid highlights.** Each month we feature the Cozy Nights Fund and other circles keeping neighbours warm, fed, and held.

## Part of Boundaries in Bloom

Our seasonal theme invites us to hold boundaries that sustain care. Calm shows us how: we practice saying “I can respond after I rest,” we prepare gentle auto-replies, and we schedule body breaks into our organizing plans. Calm is what lets boundaries stay loving rather than rigid.

## Ease your way in

Begin with the **Community Calm toolkit** download—it includes a breathing orb practice, a restorative stretch audio, and a check-in for your care pod. When you need extra tenderness, brew the Luna Larder night infusion and listen to the *Soft Shields* playlist in the curated list.

Share how you are using the Calm hub by tagging @witchclick or leaving a note in the comments. Let us know which mutual aid partners you want to spotlight next. Calm is a communal skill—we learn it together, practice it imperfectly, and keep showing up.
