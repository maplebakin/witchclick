steps:
  - title: Day One — Arrive
    description: |
      **Morning:** Brew a warm drink and journal for six minutes about how you want to feel.  
      **Midday:** Take a five-minute sensory break—wash your hands with intention, stretch, and name one texture you appreciate.  
      **Evening:** Light a candle (or turn on a soft lamp) and list three moments of calm you noticed today.
  - title: Day Two — Deepen
    description: |
      **Morning:** Choose a guiding word for the day. Draw it as a sigil on a sticky note.  
      **Midday:** Step outside or by a window for three minutes of sky-gazing. Match your breath to something you see moving.  
      **Evening:** Take a gentle bath or foot soak, thanking your body for carrying you.
  - title: Day Three — Share
    description: |
      **Morning:** Send a voice note or message to someone you trust about how you want to be supported.  
      **Midday:** Hold a grounding stone or favorite object, breathe deeply, and reaffirm your guiding word.  
      **Evening:** Reflect on the biggest shift from this sampler and note one ritual you want to keep.
