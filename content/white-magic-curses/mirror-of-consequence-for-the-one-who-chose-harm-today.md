## Opening Reflection

You are not here to make peace with what was cruel. You are here to set truth back in its seat. This working is a mirror, not a weapon: it returns what was sent, without adding harm, until accountability finds its rightful home. If your body agrees, breathe in slowly and feel the line of your spine. Let anger be a signal, not a leash. By stepping in, you consent to clarity arriving, and you consent to release your grip on the outcome.

## Invocation

May every act of harm, every lie, and every careless choice return to its rightful source, until truth is unavoidable and accountability takes root.

## Method

1. Place a small bowl of water in front of you. Light one candle, or switch on a soft lamp, to stand for steady truth.
2. Name the target by role, not by name: 'the one who harmed me' or 'the one who chose deceit.' Keep it clean.
3. Hold your hands over the water and exhale three times, imagining your energy pulling back from their field like thread slipping free.
4. Speak the invocation once. Then say, 'What is mine returns to me. What is theirs returns to them, unchanged, until it teaches.'
5. With one finger, stir the water clockwise seven times. Feel the quiet bite of consequence turning toward its source.
6. Look into the surface and picture their actions meeting themselves—doors closing where they should, truths surfacing where they hid, patterns reflecting until they are seen.
7. When you feel a shift, touch the water to your forehead, throat, and heart as a boundary seal.
8. Pour the water down the drain and blow out the candle, letting the mirror keep working without you.

## Closure & Aftercare

Seal this by placing a hand on your chest and another on your belly. Say, 'I do not carry what is not mine.' Drink a full glass of water, eat something with weight, and unclench your jaw. If your nerves are loud, play a song that makes you feel returned to yourself. Rest. The mirror knows the way back without your supervision.

## Safety Notes

Use a flameless candle if kids, pets, or tiredness are nearby.
